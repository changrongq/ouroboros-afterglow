**风格（Suno prompt）**

Mandarin acoustic indie folk ballad, gentle 6/8 sway, slow and unhurried. Warm intimate female alto vocals, clear Mandarin diction, natural conversational phrasing, restrained emotion. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar opens alone, joined by delicate felt piano. Sparse verses with breathing room between phrases. A memorable, flowing chorus with a tender melody on the repeated opening line. Soft brushed drums, warm acoustic bass and subtle vocal harmonies enter in the second chorus. Reflective, comforting and quietly hopeful, like an evening conversation beside an open window. Organic acoustic production, close vocals, gentle room reverb. A soft bridge leads into a stripped-back outro; instruments gradually fall away, leaving the final line sung alone.
